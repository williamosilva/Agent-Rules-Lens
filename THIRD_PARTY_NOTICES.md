another. Each mark is the property of its owner. They are included for
nominative identification only: this extension is not affiliated with, endorsed
by, or sponsored by any of these projects or companies.

issue and it will be replaced with the neutral `generic-agent` mark.

## Notes on licensing

- A repository licence such as Apache-2.0 or MIT covers the **file**, not the
  trademark drawn in it. The mark itself stays under its owner's brand
  guidelines in every case.
- Marks taken from Simple Icons come from a CC0-1.0 licensed collection; Simple
  Icons states that the brands themselves remain trademarks of their owners.
  See <https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/LICENSE.md>.
- Where a source offered no explicit licence, the record reads
  `Trademark asset — usage subject to the owner's brand guidelines`.

## First party marks

`shared-rules.svg`, `custom-rules.svg` and `generic-agent.svg` were drawn for
this repository. They are deliberately neutral line drawings that imitate no
commercial brand, and are covered by this project's MIT licence.

`shared-rules` is used for `AGENTS.md` and `AGENTS.override.md`, which several
tools read, so that group is never labelled with any single vendor's logo.
